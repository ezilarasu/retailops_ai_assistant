# 1. What is BM25?

## Definition

BM25 is a ranking algorithm used to retrieve documents based on keyword relevance.

It is one of the most widely used retrieval algorithms in the world.

---

Used by:

```text id="bm1"
Elasticsearch
OpenSearch
Lucene
Solr
```

---

# Why Does BM25 Exist?

Simple keyword matching is weak.

---

Example:

Query:

```text id="bm2"
P1 outage
```

---

Document A:

```text id="bm3"
P1 means full store outage.
```

---

Document B:

```text id="bm4"
Store opening checklist.
```

---

Naive matching:

```text id="bm5"
Contains words?
```

---

BM25 asks:

```text id="bm6"
How important are these words?
```

---

---

# 5. Term Frequency (TF)

## Definition

How often a word appears in a document.

---

Example

Query:

```text id="bm15"
P1
```

---

Document:

```text id="bm16"
P1 means full store outage.

P1 incidents require escalation.
```

---

Term Frequency:

```text id="bm17"
P1 appears twice
```

---

Higher TF:

```text id="bm18"
More Relevant
```

usually.

---

# Problem

Without correction:

```text id="bm19"
P1 P1 P1 P1 P1
```

would dominate.

---

BM25 prevents this.

---

# 6. Inverse Document Frequency (IDF)

## Definition

Measures how rare a term is across all documents.

---

Example:

Word:

```text id="bm20"
the
```

appears everywhere.

---

IDF:

```text id="bm21"
Low
```

---

Word:

```text id="bm22"
DENIM-4421
```

appears once.

---

IDF:

```text id="bm23"
High
```

---

Meaning:

```text id="bm24"
Rare Words
=
More Valuable
```

---

# Why This Matters

Question:

```text id="bm25"
Who owns DENIM-4421?
```

---

BM25 knows:

```text id="bm26"
DENIM-4421
```

is highly informative.

---

# 7. Document Length Normalization

## Problem

Long documents naturally contain more words.

---

Without normalization:

```text id="bm27"
Huge documents
```

would always win.

---

Example

Document A:

```text id="bm28"
50 words
```

---

Document B:

```text id="bm29"
5000 words
```

---

Long document likely contains more query terms.

---

BM25 adjusts for this.

---

Result:

```text id="bm30"
Fair Ranking
```

---
