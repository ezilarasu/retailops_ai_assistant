RetailOps IT Support Knowledge Base

POS terminal frozen:
Restart the terminal. If the issue persists, disconnect power for 30 seconds and reboot. If still unresolved, create a P1 incident.

Password reset:
Employees should use the self-service identity portal. Managers cannot reset passwords directly.

Wi-Fi outage:
Check whether back-office devices are also offline. Restart the store router only after approval from IT support.

Scanner not working:
Replace batteries, re-pair the scanner with the POS terminal, and test using a known SKU.

Incident priority:
P1 means full store outage or payment processing failure.
P2 means partial outage affecting some terminals.
P3 means single-device issue or non-urgent request.

For P1 incidents, managers must notify IT support immediately and include the store ID, device ID, and timestamp in the incident ticket.
