RetailOps Returns and Refunds Policy

Online orders may be returned within 30 days of delivery with proof of purchase.

In-store purchases may be returned within 45 days of purchase with original receipt.

Final sale items cannot be returned unless defective.

Refunds must be issued to the original payment method unless store credit is approved by a manager.

Items without receipt may be exchanged for store credit at the current selling price.

Damaged or defective items should be inspected by a manager before refund approval.

Cross-brand returns are not allowed. Items purchased from Brand A cannot be returned to Brand B stores.
