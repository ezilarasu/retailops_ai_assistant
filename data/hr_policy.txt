RetailOps HR Policy

Full-time store employees receive 18 paid vacation days per year after completing 90 days of employment.

Part-time store employees receive paid time off according to local labor regulations and average weekly hours.

Remote work is not available for store associates, cashiers, stockroom associates, or store managers because these roles require physical store presence.

Corporate employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Sick leave must be reported at least 2 hours before the scheduled shift when possible.

Employees must complete annual compliance training by October 31 each year.

All harassment, discrimination, or workplace safety concerns must be reported through HR Connect or directly to the district HR partner.
