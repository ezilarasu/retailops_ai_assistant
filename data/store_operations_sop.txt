RetailOps Store Operations SOP

Store opening checklist:
1. Disable alarm.
2. Verify POS terminals are online.
3. Count opening cash drawer.
4. Inspect fitting rooms.
5. Confirm emergency exits are clear.
6. Review daily staffing plan.

Inventory mismatch process:
If physical inventory does not match system inventory, the associate must recount the item, check recent transfers, review damaged goods logs, and notify the store operations manager.

Customer escalation process:
If a customer becomes upset, the associate should remain calm, listen actively, apologize for the inconvenience, and involve the manager on duty if the issue cannot be resolved within 5 minutes.

POS outage process:
If POS systems are offline for more than 10 minutes, the manager must contact IT support, open an incident ticket, and switch to manual transaction logging if approved.
